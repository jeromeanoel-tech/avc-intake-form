Re: Engagement Agreement for Veteran Advocacy and Support Services

Dear ,

Thank you for choosing the Australian Veteran Centre (AVC) to assist you with your Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) and Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation (CSC) matters. This letter sets out the scope of our services, terms of engagement, and your rights and responsibilities.

1. Scope of Services

AVC will act as your authorised advocate and representative for:

  • DVA Initial Liability and Permanent Impairment claims
  • CSC matters relating to invalidity classifications, medical reviews, reclassifications, and associated entitlements
  • Gathering evidence, preparing submissions, and managing your claim
  • Liaising with DVA, CSC, and medical providers on your behalf

We do not provide medical, legal, or financial planning advice. If specialist input is needed, we will refer you to the appropriate professionals.

2. Engagement Period

This engagement commences on and continues until:

  • Your claim and/or appeal process concludes, or
  • Either party terminates the engagement in accordance with Section 6 below.

3. Fees and Payment

Our service fee is 4.5% of:

  • Any Permanent Impairment lump-sum payments made by DVA, and
  • Any CSC back-pay entitlements arising from DVA liability decisions.

AVC does not charge fees on dependant benefits or family-member entitlements.

When payment occurs: You will receive an invoice before your compensation or back-pay is released. Payment is due within 7 days of receiving funds.

If you end the engagement before DVA accepts liability, AVC may charge a reasonable fee for the work completed to that point.

If you end the engagement after your claim reaches the Permanent Impairment assessment stage and prior to a Permanent Impairment determination, the 4.5% fee (excluding GST) remains payable after determination, as the work required to reach that stage has already been completed.

All fees are subject to GST.

4. Confidentiality and Privacy

We maintain strict confidentiality of all information provided during this engagement. Information will only be disclosed:

  • With your written consent,
  • As required by law, or
  • As necessary to fulfil our obligations to DVA, CSC, or medical providers.

All client records are securely stored in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

5. Limitation of Liability

While all reasonable care will be taken in representing you, our total liability for any claim arising from this engagement — whether in contract, tort, or otherwise — shall be limited to the total amount of fees paid for our services. We are not liable for delays or decisions made by DVA, CSC, or other agencies beyond our control.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 days' written notice.

Upon termination for any other reason, you remain liable for fees due for work completed prior to the termination date.

7. Independence and Conduct

The Australian Veteran Centre is an independent, veteran-owned organisation. We are not affiliated with DVA, the Commonwealth Government, or any government body. Our role is to advocate for your interests professionally, ethically, and without conflict.

8. Acceptance of Terms

Please confirm your acceptance of these terms by signing below. If you have any questions about the terms or scope of this engagement, please contact us directly before signing.

We look forward to supporting you through your DVA and CSC processes.

Kind regards,

Justin Caboche
Director, Australian Veteran Centre
